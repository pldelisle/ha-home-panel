# Home panel: what's tappable, measured against Apple's guidelines

Nov 17, 2026 · main at `bc650e0` (after #368, #369 and #372)

**Bottom line: 65/100, "systematic rework" on the template's scale.** Sizes and visible text pass, but the panel's two big
departures from Apple are about what's tappable. First, Liquid Glass covers the content layer: every card, and since #368
every raised control inside a card, is glass. Apple keeps glass for controls and navigation that float above content.
Second, the same capsule and tile shapes mean "button" in one place and "status" in another, and five things act on a tap
without being buttons, or are buttons inside buttons. Twelve refactors follow, the first six about clickability.

## How it was checked

Every tab of the test house on a 1180×820 iPad and a 393×852 iPhone, light and dark (40 views), was walked by a
Playwright script that lists every element that reacts to a tap (its size, accessible name, fill, shadow, border and
chevron), every element that only shows a pointer, buttons nested in buttons, and glass outside navigation. All the text
on screen was checked for low-contrast colours. Colour pairs and sizes were scored with the HIG skill's `hig_checker.py`.
The rules were read from the HIG pages themselves (Materials, Buttons, Toggles, Lists and tables, Layout, Accessibility,
Segmented controls, Sheets). The test house uses made-up data. Tags: 🟢 measured, 🟡 seen on screenshots and needs a check
on the wall iPad, 🔴 judgement.

## Smaller notes

- Scene tiles run a scene on tap but look exactly like the Home tiles that open a section. Apple's buttons carry their role
  in their style: a run action could lead with a tinted icon circle like the light cells, so "does" and "opens" differ. 🔴
- Two colour tokens fail 4.5:1 but no visible text uses them: `label` (3.65:1 on `bg`) and `fg3` (3.38:1 on `card`). Keep
  them off text or darken them before someone does. 🟢
- Nothing in the panel copies Apple's assets: the icons are the panel's own, the switch is 56×30 (not the iPhone's 51×31),
  and the font stacks name no Apple face. 🟢

## What should change in the design rules

`docs/design.md` (from #368) says a control is "raised glass". Apple says the opposite for the content layer, so item 1
should rewrite it: **content is opaque, navigation floats in glass, and a filled capsule always means a button.** Items 4, 5
and 7 then follow from that one sentence, and the design system's Surfaces section should be updated with it.

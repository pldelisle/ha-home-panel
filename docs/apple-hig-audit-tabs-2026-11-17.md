# Every tab, measured against Apple's guidelines

Nov 17, 2026 · `claude/hig-content-opaque` at `892fc50` (after the opaque content layer, the Files-style sidebar and the Car rework)

**Bottom line: 70/100, "fix before release" on the template's scale. This morning's audit gave 65.** Three things are now
right: the content layer is opaque, the sidebar follows Files, and Car follows the HIG. The biggest remaining problem is a
bug, not a design choice: **on a phone, every sheet opened from a tab is cut off** (finding 1, a one-word fix). After that,
the panel is held back by navigation more than by visuals:

- the phone tab bar hides "More";
- two phone tabs (Air, Energy) are a card that opens a sheet;
- every tab repeats a greeting and a second title above its own;
- the same kind of number opens its history in one tab but not in the next.

Fixing those makes the panel simpler, and it also makes Home Assistant's data easier to reach.

## How it was checked

- **Measurements.** Every tab of the test house on a 1180×820 iPad and a 393×852 iPhone, light and dark (40 views), was
  walked by the same Playwright script as this morning. It lists every element that reacts to a tap (size, accessible
  name, fill, chevron), elements that only show a pointer, buttons nested in buttons, and glass outside navigation.
- **Sheets.** The More sheet, a Climate room sheet and Settings were opened on the iPhone and screenshotted. The sheet
  bug was traced to its rule and the fix was tried by injecting one style (no code changed).
- **Scoring.** Colour pairs and sizes were scored with the HIG skill's `hig_checker.py`.
- **Sources.** The rules come from the HIG pages saved this morning (listed under Sources).
- **Data.** The test house uses made-up data: the sine-wave charts and the empty Energy history are the house, not the
  panel.
- **Tags.** 🟢 measured, 🟡 seen on screenshots and needs a check on the wall iPad, 🔴 judgement.

### Smaller notes

- **Home, iPad.** The weather card also holds Who's home and Now playing: three unrelated things in one box. Split them
  into the weather, then people, then media (Now playing could join the tile grid). The tiles still have the boxed "›"
  (morning 5); use a plain trailing chevron as the light cells do. 🔴
- **Home tiles' names** include the "›" glyph ("Lights › 4 on…"), so VoiceOver reads it. Mark the chevron
  `aria-hidden`. 🟢
- **Climate, iPad.** The selected room has a blue outline. Lists select with a filled background, as the rail now does
  (`--nav-sel-bg`). 🔴
- **Weather, iPhone.** The hero's row (Wind, Humidity, UV, Air quality) is repeated by the tiles right under it. Keep the
  tiles; drop the row. 🟢
- **Air, iPad.** The "Outdoor air, last 24 hours" title sits against the pollen tiles with no gap. The dual-axis chart (US
  AQI and AQHI) is hard to read, and its markers are clipped at the top: two small charts, or AQHI as a line on its own
  scale. 🟢🟡
- **Security.** "Add UniFi Protect" reads as plain text in a well; give it the bordered button look. 🟢
- **The dark error toast** is still white on `#da8282` (2.8:1, morning 12): `--crit` in dark mode → about `#b34a4a`. 🟢
- **iPhone, the ☰ button** opens Home Assistant's own sidebar. It's the platform's way out of the panel, so it stays. It
  could live in Settings to make room for the title, but on a wall iPad it's rarely wanted. 🔴
- **iPad keyboard.** No shortcuts. If the panel is ever used on a desk, ⌘1–⌘9 for the tabs and Esc already works. 🔴

## What should change in the design rules

`docs/design.md` should say five things it doesn't yet:

- A value tile with a chevron opens that value's history, everywhere (6).
- A tab has one range control (7).
- Status is text with a dot, never a filled capsule (9).
- Every time and date goes through `fmt` (11).
- Labels are sentence case (15).

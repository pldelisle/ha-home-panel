# Home panel: what still disagrees with itself, measured against Apple's guidelines

Nov 17, 2026 · main at `383b2dd` (after epic #376 and the HIG follow-ups #393 to #400)

**Bottom line: 73/100, "fix before release" on the template's scale (up from 65).** Epic #376 fixed what was tappable:
glass is off the content layer, status reads as text with a dot, and no button sits inside another. What is left is
**consistency**. The same fact is drawn two ways (Weather on the phone, the TV's power), the same number is written to
two precisions (64% and 64.0%), and some numbers on one screen contradict each other. Seventeen items follow. The first
six cover most of the gap.

## How it was checked

Every tab of the test house on a 1180×820 iPad and a 393×852 iPhone, light and dark, plus each sheet a tap opens on the
iPad (32 sheets) and the Settings popup. A temporary Playwright walk took the screenshots and listed every control under
44 pt, text under 11 pt, uppercase labels, glass surfaces, unnamed controls and buttons nested in buttons. Colour pairs
and sizes were scored with the HIG skill's `hig_checker.py` (60/100 on the four measurable failures). axe already gates
serious findings in CI (`test/e2e/a11y.spec.ts`), so this pass looked at what axe cannot see. The test house uses
made-up data. Tags: 🟢 measured or read in the code, 🟡 seen on the test house and needs a check on the live house, 🔴
judgement.

## What passed this time

- No button inside another, and every card and control is named (#389). 🟢
- Glass is on the rail, the tab bar, sheets, dialogs and toasts only. Cards keep a near-opaque fill with a plain blur, as
  `docs/design.md` describes. 🟢
- Every control is 44 pt or larger, apart from the two departure fields. The switch is drawn 56×30, and its `::before`
  pads the tap area to 44 pt. 🟢
- No uppercase spaced labels left (#390). Status is text with a dot (#393). Dark mode holds 4.5:1 on every token pair
  checked. 🟢
- The confirmation dialogs (Unlock, Set all, All off) are real alerts with Cancel on the leading side (#377, #378). Unlock
  could take the destructive style, since it opens the car to anyone. 🔴
